Why it exists

Today the brand is rebuilt by hand - every time.

Every team restyles from scratch. Colours drift, type wanders, spacing is guessed. A screen shipped this month doesn't match one from last. Each team must handle consistency and accessibility alone.

Multiply that across every team, app, and deck, and the firm looks less like one firm.

The shift

You inherit the brand.

Declare who you are - your team, and whether it's a public-facing page or an internal tool. The system resolves every colour, typeface, space, and state. On brand, by default.

Quality, built in

Accessible and correct - by default.

  • ✓Legible for everyone. Contrast is verified at build time - the defaults are tested and pass WCAG AA.
  • ✓Works everywhere. Older machines, locked-down browsers, PDF exports - colour degrades cleanly instead of breaking.
  • ✓Prints cleanly. A dark or brand page prints black-on-white automatically - not washed out or blank.
  • ✓Reads in any language. Layouts mirror for right-to-left scripts without a redesign.
