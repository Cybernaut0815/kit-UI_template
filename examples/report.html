Method and assumptions

Illuminance is computed hourly for every grid point with a Perez sky from the weather file. Daylight autonomy (DA) is the share of occupied hours a point reaches 300 lx on daylight alone; spatial daylight autonomy (sDA 300/50) is the share of a zone's points with a DA of at least 50%.

Blinds are not modelled and neighbouring buildings are taken from the site model as delivered. Results for the core assume the light well finishes at a reflectance of 0.7.
