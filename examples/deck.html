kit-ui

Sharp tools,
one look.

One stylesheet gives every kit module - simulation, generation, visualization - the same corners, type, colour and states - so a new tool looks finished on its first commit.

Scroll, or use the arrow keys.

Why

Every module was styling itself by hand.

Each generator, mesh editor and viewer grew its own colours, radii and button rules. They drifted, and each one carried its own accessibility debt. Now they share one vocabulary of tokens and classes.

Four switches

Declare it once on <html>.

data-theme

dark · light

Dark by default; without the attribute the page follows the system.

data-surface

brand

Black-first for decks and heroes, in both themes.

data-density

compact

Controls and rows from 44 to 36 px for dense tools.

data-kit

sim · forge · viz

One preset per product family; forge by default.

The ramp

Orange, red, burgundy, purple.

Gradients are decoration only: heroes, decks, identity. Work surfaces get the quiet .grad-wash and .grad-glow.

Three kits

One system, three families.

data-kit="sim"

kit-sim

Simulation - daylight, energy, flow. Orange into red.

data-kit="forge"

kit-forge

Generation - parcels, plans, meshes. Red into burgundy.

data-kit="viz"

kit-viz

Visualization - viewers, charts, reports. Purple into burgundy.

Two surfaces

Present in black. Work in light.

data-surface="brand"

Presentation

Black-first, bright accent under ink text.

data-theme="light" island

Work

Any element carrying its own data-theme escapes the page.

Rules

Four rules keep it one system.

  1. Tokens only. No hex, no invented variables - every colour and size already exists.
  2. Square by default. --radius is 0; soften a page with one variable, never per component.
  3. No build. The css/ folder is the package; link it and go.
  4. Accessible by construction. A contrast gate checks every theme and kit on every change.

Start

One line of HTML.

<link rel="stylesheet" href="./node_modules/kit-ui/css/kit-ui.css">

See every token and primitive or open the app example.
