Sharp by default.

One stylesheet for every kit module - kit-sim, kit-forge, kit-viz: square corners, Geist type and a warm ramp from orange over red and burgundy to purple. Flip the switches below - every section re-resolves live.

Colour

The warm ramp - 400 bright (dark-theme fills), 500 vivid (gradients), 600 deep (light-theme fills). Burgundy carries the gradients from red into purple. The kit picks the accent: data-kit="sim" orange, forge red (default), viz purple.
